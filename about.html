About Us

Pool

Our pool is a great place for dogs to cool off and have fun. It is designed with safety in mind and is supervised by trained staff at all times.

Play Area

Our play area is a spacious and secure environment where dogs can socialize and play with other dogs. It is equipped with various toys and obstacles to keep them entertained.

Agility Course

Our agility course is designed to challenge and stimulate dogs both mentally and physically. It includes a variety of obstacles such as tunnels, jumps, and weave poles.
